Somewhere along the way, design became separated from code. Designers drew mockups in Figma, handed them over the fence, and hoped engineers could figure out the interactions.

I never believed in that divide.

With over 7 years of working experience as an AI-Product Engineer and Senior UI/UX Designer, I design experiences that convert and write the production code that brings them to life. From ambient AI healthcare platforms and nationwide customs portals to full-stack Next.js applications, I build software that looks exceptional and performs flawlessly under real-world scale.

For me, digital craftsmanship has always been about one thing: does it work? Does it create real value for users? Does it drive measurable business results?
