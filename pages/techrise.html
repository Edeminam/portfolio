Engineering Overview

The Mission & Technical Architecture

TechRise Digital Transformation Initiative (DTI) is an African pan-continental educational movement driving grassroots empowerment through modern technology literacy, software engineering training, and digital skills acceleration.

As an AI-Product Engineer, Emmanuel was responsible for the entire end-to-end delivery: from user experience design systems to writing the production frontend codebase. The platform needed to cater to students accessing content from a wide spectrum of devices and fluctuating cellular network bandwidths across Nigeria and beyond.

Emmanuel engineered the platform with Next.js and Tailwind CSS, implementing aggressive static site generation (SSG), lightweight vector assets, accessible semantic markup, and an interactive course discovery module that lets prospective learners explore technical tracks and register effortlessly.
