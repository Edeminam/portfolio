Overview

The Brief & Concept

Spring Cakes Studio, based in Stockport, Greater Manchester, crafts bespoke wedding cakes and luxury celebration confections for discerning clients across the UK. Despite an ardent local following and artisanal mastery, their digital presence did not reflect the luxury standard of their creations.

Ordering bespoke cakes is notoriously prone to customer hesitation: picking sponge tiers, intricate frosting styles, delivery slots, and dietary requirements traditionally required lengthy back-and-forth emails. Emmanuel designed an interactive, sensory digital storefront featuring a multi-step custom cake builder.

Clients can now personalize their dream cake, see instant pricing updates, schedule delicate refrigerated delivery, and pay with Apple Pay or credit card in under two minutes.

Live Site — Design Screens
Homepage & Storefront

Hero & Catalogue Discovery

A bold, sensory homepage anchored by the brand tagline "Delicious Custom Cakes for Every Occasion". The full-bleed hero image leads into a curated catalogue grid — Birthday, Signature & Novelty, Special Occasion, and Wedding — providing instant visual category navigation. Trustpilot social proof is embedded above the fold, and a WhatsApp CTA floats for instant personal engagement.

Catalogue & Filtering

Category Catalogue — Birthday Cakes

A refined product listing page with persistent left-rail filtering by category, tag (Barbie, Batman, Chanel, etc.), and a live search bar. The grid layout gives each cake equal visual weight with name and price range displayed beneath — reducing decision fatigue and guiding customers from browse to intent without friction.

Product Detail

Product Page & Size Configurator

A focused product detail view for the Tiffany Inspired Bow Pendant Cake. Prominent imagery, real-time pricing by size (6", 8", 10" tiers), stock badge, and a cake serving guide expand-panel. A "You May Also Like" cross-sell row drives AOV. The WhatsApp CTA keeps direct order enquiries frictionless for high-intent buyers.

Custom Order Flow

Bespoke Cake Order Request Form

The centrepiece of the acquisition strategy — a multi-field custom cake configurator capturing occasion type, sponge flavour, icing preference, message, size, quantity, and image reference uploads. Structured to convert browsing customers into confirmed bespoke orders with guided, low-friction inputs rather than open email threads.
