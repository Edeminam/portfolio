MYKA

On-Demand Digital Insurance & Instant Claims Platform

Insurtech Fintech Web & Mobile Product Design
Visit Live Site (myka.ng) ↗
Overview

The Brief & Product Thesis

Traditional insurance in Nigeria has suffered from deep consumer friction: opaque policy wording, multi-week claims verifications, and cumbersome paperwork. Myka set out to reinvent insurance for digital-first Africans by offering flexible, bite-sized monthly protection for everyday smartphones, laptops, vehicles, and health emergencies.

Emmanuel crafted the consumer mobile-web experience from scratch. The design stripped out confusing legal jargon in favor of plain-language coverage cards, transparent deductibles, and a rapid 3-step quotation engine that issues active policy numbers in under 3 minutes.

For claims, Emmanuel designed an intuitive photo-upload questionnaire that lets users document damage in real time and monitor review status via live push and SMS notifications.
