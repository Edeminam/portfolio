Overview

The Brief & Product Thesis

Traditional insurance across Nigeria has suffered from chronic consumer friction: sub-1% penetration across a population of over 200 million people, deeply rooted distrust, opaque paper contracts, and multi-week claim settlements. Myka set out to reinvent the insurance experience for modern Africans through transparent, on-demand micro-policies covering smartphones, laptops, motor vehicles, health, and education.

As Lead Product Designer, Emmanuel architected the end-to-end web and mobile ecosystem from first principles. By replacing dense legal language with plain-spoken coverage cards, transparent deductibles, and a rapid 3-step quotation engine, users can compare verified underwriting quotes and activate an official policy in under 3 minutes.

Alongside the consumer platform, he designed Sell with Myka, a partner mobile app that lets agents and businesses quote and sell health, motor, property, term life, savings and education plans, verify clients with their NIN, get certified through Myka Academy, and manage daily collections and commissions.

The platform also bridges the consumer-enterprise divide with a dedicated B2B corporate suite (Myka for Corporates), allowing businesses to manage employee group life, HMO health schemes, and company assets through a unified administrative experience.

01 — Market Challenge & Transformation

Dismantling the Nigerian Insurance Trust Deficit

For decades, insurance products in Nigeria were distributed through high-pressure physical brokers and complex clauses that left policyholders unsure of what was actually protected when emergencies occurred.

Legacy Insurance Friction

The Traditional Insurance Reality

  • Intimidating Legal Jargon: 20+ page paper policies filled with obscure exclusion clauses and hidden deductibles.
  • Lump-Sum Annual Burdens: Rigid annual premium requirements that exclude everyday earners and gig workers.
  • Slow Physical Processing: In-person branch inspections, manual forms, and weeks of back-and-forth claims verification.
  • Zero Digital Self-Service: Inability to check policy status, renew online, or upload incident photos directly.
The Myka Digital Thesis

Human-Centered Insurtech Design

  • Plain-Language Clarity: Clear, visual policy summaries explaining exactly what is covered and what is not.
  • "Pay Small Small" Micro-Premiums: Flexible monthly plans starting from ₦1,000/month for smartphone screen & gadget cover.
  • 3-Minute Instant Issuance: Direct digital binding with leading underwriters like AXA, AIICO, Custodian, and AVON HMO.
  • Instant Photo Claims Engine: 100% digital damage documentation with live status tracking and prompt payouts.
02 — Web Platform Interfaces

Desktop Architecture & Core Discovery Flows

Clean, accessible desktop layouts designed to inspire immediate trust, streamline category selection, and bridge individual policyholders with regulated underwriter networks.

Web Storefront & Hero Experience

Hero Value Proposition & Underwriter Credibility

The primary landing experience is anchored by the reassuring brand promise: "Tomorrow is safe with us." A floating pill navigation bar provides quick access to Claims, Policies, Corporate Solutions, and instant sign-in. Prominent hero feature banners highlight bite-sized gadget insurance starting from ₦1,000 monthly with clear 'Protect My Phone' action triggers. Directly beneath, official NAICOM broker licensing badges and an underwriter marquee (Custodian, AIICO, AVON HMO, AXA Mansard, Bastion, Casava, Cornerstone, Coronation) establish institutional authority and consumer confidence above the fold.

Interactive Policy Selector

Multi-Vertical Insurance Product Matrix

A personalized, conversational selector greets visitors ("Good evening, What would you like Myka to insure today?") and presents accessible coverage categories. Custom isometric iconography guides users between Motor Insurance tiers (Third Party, Advanced, Comprehensive), Health Insurance (Individual & Group Health), and Education Savings plans (Myka Edu Assured, Myka Eduflex). Each card features clear 'Popular' badges and instant quotation CTAs to eliminate decision paralysis.

B2B Enterprise Solutions

Corporate Coverage & Employee Benefits

Engineered for Nigerian enterprises and fast-growing startups seeking dependable protection: "Insurance for Nigerian Businesses That Actually Works." The corporate landing experience highlights customized group health HMOs, asset protection, fleet insurance, and executive consultation booking with NAICOM-certified brokers, reducing administrative friction for corporate HR and finance teams.

Brand Narrative & Transparency

Human-Centered Insurance Storytelling

Directly confronting Nigeria's cultural insurance skepticism: "Insurance that finally works. Myka exists to fix a problem every Nigerian understands." Warm, authentic team photography and clear editorial messaging explain how Myka acts as an impartial consumer advocate and licensed broker—ensuring legitimate claims are paid swiftly without bureaucratic excuses.

03 — Sell with Myka: Agent App

A Mobile Storefront for Insurance Agents

Beyond the consumer web platform, Emmanuel designed the Myka partner app, a mobile toolkit that lets agents, dealerships and field partners sell policies, onboard clients and track earnings from one place.

Partner Home & Catalogue

Everything an Agent Sells, One Scroll Away

The home screen greets the partner by business name and puts total commission and available balance in a bold orange card, followed by quick actions to Sell Policy, Manage Earnings and Manage Agents. Below, a NAICOM-licensed "Sell with confidence" banner builds trust, and the catalogue groups Term Life, Savings & Investment, Education Savings, Annuity, Health, Motor and Property products into scannable rows.

Field Work Planning

"Which Cluster Are You Working?"

For collection agents, the home screen surfaces today's plan: overdue and paid-today counts, a See who to visit shortcut, and a bottom sheet for choosing which market cluster to work that day.

Help Centre

Find Answers Quickly

A navy help centre with a friendly "Hello Emmanuel, how can we help?" greeting, searchable articles, and collections covering getting started, policies, commissions and Myka Academy.

04 — Client Onboarding & KYC

Verified Clients in a Few Taps

Every sale begins by capturing the client correctly. The flow keeps the form short and uses the national identity database to fill in the rest.

Client Details & NIN Check

NAICOM-Compliant Identity Verification

Agents enter a client's NIN, address, phone and email with optional fields clearly marked. The app then returns a verified identity card (name, date of birth, gender, address) for the agent to confirm, with a Re-enter details fallback and a privacy note explaining that the NIN is only used for verification.

05 — Property Insurance Flow

From Risk Check to Payment

Property cover is priced from a handful of plain-language questions, then compared across underwriters side by side.

Quote Builder

Property Risk Check & Plan Comparison

Chip-based questions capture building material, roof type and safety measures (CCTV, fire extinguisher, smoke detector), followed by content and high-value item totals. Underwriter cards then show what is covered (fire, burglary, gadget cover, building damage) and the yearly premium with a single Select This Plan action.

Review & Pay

Review Your Coverage

A clean summary lists property type, occupancy and underwriter, with the total premium in a highlighted card before the payment sheet slides up.

Confirmation

Payment Successful

Success screens show the policy reference and any remaining activation steps, such as a property photo inspection, so nothing gets missed after checkout.

06 — Motor Insurance Flow

Plate Number In, Policy Out

Motor cover is the most frequently sold product, so the flow is built for speed at the roadside or the dealership.

Vehicle Identification

Plate Number or VIN

Agents enter a licence plate in a realistic plate field or switch to VIN capture, then pick the vehicle type from illustrated chips and confirm make, model and year from a searchable list.

Plans & Underwriters

Compare Cover and Payment Frequency

A navy quote card shows vehicle value and annual premium, with annual, semi-annual, quarterly and monthly options and the agent's commission shown in green. Underwriter plans expand to show exactly what is covered.

Checkout

Transparent Checkout

The payment summary shows the customer, vehicle value, sum insured and frequency, along with important disclosures on instalment rules, before confirming payment and listing the activation steps.

07 — Health, Savings & Education Plans

One Pattern, Many Products

Health, savings and education plans reuse the same building blocks (plan card, what's covered, eligibility, summary), so agents learn the pattern once and can sell every product.

Health Insurance

HMOs and Nearby Hospitals

After verification, agents choose from partner HMOs and compare cover levels (Life Plus, Premium Life, Executive Boss), and can search nearby hospitals so clients know where they can be treated.

Plan Summary

Family Health Plan Activation

A summary card shows the plan, premium and duration, followed by a confirmation screen listing activation steps (NIN, adding members) and what each member must provide.

Education Savings

Myka Edu Flex & Edu Assured

Navy projection cards show estimated maturity payouts and life cover next to monthly contributions, with frequency toggles, duration steppers, an eligibility check, and quotes that can be shared as a PDF or on WhatsApp.

Savings Plans

Select a Plan

Myka Savers, Allure and Caretaker plans are shown as comparable cards, and each plan explains in plain language what it is, what is covered and the included term life benefits.

Payment Summary

Policy Activation in Progress

The payment summary shows maturity value, recurring contribution and life cover, and the agent's earnings on the sale. A friendly progress screen then explains when the certificate will arrive.

08 — Myka Academy

Certification Before Commission

To sell responsibly, agents must be certified for each product. Myka Academy turns that requirement into short, gamified learning.

Learning Path

Get Certified to Sell

Each certification shows the time commitment, lessons, final exam and reward up front. Modules unlock in sequence, from Insurance Fundamentals through Understanding Risks to the Sales Simulator and exam, with short videos and XP rewards along the way.

Concept Checks

Quizzes With Instant Feedback

Flashcards explain a concept like Fire Damage with a real-world example, then a quick quiz confirms understanding, with green "Correct! +20 XP" and red "Almost there" states.

Sales Simulator

Practise the Pitch

A chat-based simulator plays a hesitant customer ("Property insurance just feels too expensive right now"). Agents pick a response and get feedback on whether they made an "Excellent Pitch" or lost the deal.

Exam & Certificate

Exam Passed!

Passing the exam unlocks a shareable Myka Academy certificate and the right to start selling that product.

09 — Collections, Reports & Earnings

Tools for Daily Field Collections

Many Myka customers save in small daily or weekly amounts. The collections tools help agents know who to visit, what is owed, and how the day went.

Customer Register

Who Owes, Who Is On Track

A searchable customer list shows each client's plan, amount and status (overdue or on track) with missed-cycle warnings. Filters cover branch, contribution status, product and cycle, and a Send account number sheet shares payment details on WhatsApp.

Customer Detail

Helping Customers Pay

Each customer profile shows compliance, missed cycles and recent collection attempts, plus clear guidance for helping the customer pay by bank transfer or at a POS. The daily collection report summarises what was due, paid and unpaid.

Reports

How Today Is Going

An at-a-glance card shows today's collections and compliance rate, a seven-day bar chart tracks performance, and downloadable daily, overdue and commission reports support branch reviews.

Account Opening

Hauwa's Account Is Open

New customers get a working account number right away, with a WhatsApp welcome message preview. An end-of-day "How the day went" screen breaks down collected money and who is still unpaid.

Earnings

Earnings You Can See

A navy earnings wallet shows the available balance and pending clearance, a one-tap Withdraw to bank button, and a breakdown of commission by product and period.

10 — Design System & Brand Tokens

Visual Identity, Color Hierarchy & Tone of Voice

A balanced design language pairing energetic, approachable safety accents with authoritative financial stability.

Brand Palette

Curated Color System

  • Safety Orange (#E86326): The primary brand accent, conveying energy, protection, and high-visibility CTAs.
  • Midnight Navy (#1C274C): Evoking corporate governance, regulatory stability, and crisp heading contrast.
  • Ice Blue Canvas (#F4F7FB): A soothing, modern background surface that prevents eye fatigue across dense policy tables.
  • Pure White & Slate: High-contrast card containers meeting strict WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
Experience Principles

Tone & Interaction Model

  • Friendly & Plain-Spoken: Replacing complex legal terms with relatable concepts like "Pay small small" and "Cracked screen sorted".
  • Context-Aware Microcopy: Time-sensitive friendly greetings ("Good evening, What would you like Myka to insure today?").
  • Instant Human Escalation: Embedded one-tap WhatsApp integration for policyholders desiring direct voice or chat assurance.
  • Instant Cryptographic Policy ID: Immediately downloadable and shareable PDF coverage documents upon checkout completion.
