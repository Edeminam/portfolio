Overview

The Brief & Challenge

The Federal Ministry of Finance and Nigeria Customs Service oversee hundreds of billions in annual import duty exemptions granted to strategic capital investments, healthcare equipment, and agricultural machinery. Prior to the project, the workflow required paper submissions, physical clearance dossiers across multiple ministries, and weeks of unmonitored review times.

As Lead UI/UX Designer, Emmanuel was tasked with transforming this complex multi-stakeholder ecosystem into a unified, secure digital portal. The solution needed to enforce strict compliance verification, provide applicants with real-time SLA tracking, and equip customs officers at border checkpoints with cryptographic QR code validation.

The result is a streamlined experience that cuts administrative friction, eliminates paperwork backlogs, and establishes an auditable digital trail for every single certificate issued.
