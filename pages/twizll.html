TWIZLL

African Fashion Marketplace — Buyers Mobile App for iOS & Android

Fashion E-Commerce iOS & Android Mobile Product Design
Overview

The Brief & Product Thesis

Twizll is a fashion marketplace built to connect shoppers with Africa's finest and most carefully curated designs. The platform brings three communities into a single ecosystem: buyers who shop collections and track deliveries, sellers who launch their own e-commerce storefronts to a global audience, and stylists who curate unique looks, share them with the world, and earn from them.

Emmanuel designed the Buyers Mobile App end to end — more than 95 screens for iOS and Android — covering onboarding, role selection, seasonal collection discovery, category browsing, product and stylist detail, cart, checkout, order tracking, error recovery, and account management.

The experience pairs an editorial, image-led browsing style with a bold coral-and-black identity, so shopping feels like flipping through a fashion magazine while still getting to checkout in a few taps.

01 — Challenge & Approach

Taking African Fashion From Scattered Feeds to One Storefront

Independent African designers often sell through social media DMs and fragmented pages, so buyers struggle to discover, compare, and trust what they're buying. Twizll set out to give that creativity a proper home.

Shopping Friction

The Fragmented Reality

  • Hard Discovery: Great designers are buried in social feeds with no categories, filters, or collections.
  • Low Trust: Missing product details, sizing, sourcing, and return policies make buyers hesitant.
  • No Order Visibility: Shoppers have no clear way to follow an order after paying.
  • No Styling Guidance: Buyers see individual pieces but rarely complete, curated looks.
The Twizll Thesis

A Curated, Editorial Marketplace

  • Seasonal Collections: Summer and Winter collection hubs with visual category tiles for fast browsing.
  • Transparent Product Pages: Size pickers, description, source, and return policy on every item.
  • Stylist-Curated Looks: Follow stylists and shop their complete outfits in one place.
  • Live Order Tracking: A clear confirmed → shipped → delivered timeline with full payment breakdowns.
02 — Onboarding & Role Selection

One App, Three Ways to Twizll

Illustrated onboarding explains the value for every audience before users choose how they want to take part.

Splash & Onboarding

Illustrated Value Carousel

A hand-lettered Twizll wordmark opens into a three-step carousel — Shop With Ease, Be A Stylist, and Own A Store. Line-art illustrations on coral and black canvases introduce each role, with a skip option and a single clear "Get Started" action.

Role Selection

"What Will You Be Doing Today?"

A single friendly question routes users into the right experience: Join as a Buyer, Become a Twizll Seller, or Become a Twizll Stylist. Each option pairs a custom icon with a full-width coral button placed within easy thumb reach.

03 — Discovery & Product Experience

Editorial Browsing That Converts

Large editorial imagery, seasonal collections, and a floating basket button keep shoppers inspired and one tap from checkout.

Seasonal Collections

Collection Hubs

Each collection opens with a full-bleed editorial hero, a filter shortcut, and cut-out category tiles — Sweaters & Cardigans, Coats, Hoodies, Scarves — so shoppers can scan a whole season at a glance.

Home & Categories

Deals, Recommendations & Product Detail

The home feed combines a collections carousel, Top Deals tiles with discount labels, and "Recommended For You" picks. Product pages lead with a swipeable gallery, clear pricing, and a tappable size selector.

Listings & Stylist Looks

Shoppable Grids & Curated Outfits

Dense three-column grids with wishlist hearts and strike-through pricing make comparing easy. Stylist looks show the creator's profile with a follow button, style description, and return policy, plus side-by-side "Add to Cart" and "Buy Now" actions.

Orders & Tracking

Transparent Order Tracking

Orders are split into Active, Completed, and Inactive tabs, with an illustrated empty state that nudges users back to shopping. Track Order shows a clear step-by-step timeline alongside the shipping address and a full payment breakdown including shipping, discount, and VAT.

04 — Cart, Edge Cases & Account

Designing for Every State

Empty carts, failed payments, and account settings got the same care as the happy path, so users are never left at a dead end.

Cart & Error Recovery

Helpful Empty & Error States

An empty cart becomes a discovery surface with Recently Viewed items and Most Viewed Categories. Payment failures use a friendly character illustration and plain-language guidance instead of a cryptic error code.

Account

Simple Account Management

A profile header sits above grouped "My Twizll Details" — Shipping Address, Messages, Saved Items, Payment Method — and "Settings & Reviews" with Change Password and Switch Account, making it easy to move between buyer, seller, and stylist roles.

05 — Visual Language

Coral, Black & Editorial Photography

A confident, fashion-forward identity that puts the clothes first and keeps actions unmistakable.

Brand Palette

Colour System

  • Twizll Coral: The signature accent for primary buttons, prices, active tabs, and the floating basket.
  • Runway Black: Headers, the splash screen, and onboarding canvases for a premium, high-contrast frame.
  • Clean White Surfaces: Neutral product cards that let fabrics, colours, and photography stand out.
Experience Principles

Interaction Model

  • Image-First Browsing: Editorial heroes and cut-out product tiles that feel like a fashion magazine.
  • Always-Reachable Basket: A floating cart button available on every browsing screen.
  • Illustrated Guidance: Custom line art and characters for onboarding, empty states, and errors.
  • Native on Both Platforms: Layouts designed for both iOS and Android conventions.
