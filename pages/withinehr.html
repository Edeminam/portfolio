Overview

The Brief & Clinical Challenge

Medical practitioners, psychologists, and outpatient clinic teams spend over 40% of their workday wrestling with antiquated, clunky electronic health record systems — leading to widespread physician burnout and diminished face-to-face patient connection.

WithinEHR integrates ambient AI audio intelligence to automatically generate accurate SOAP notes, ICD-10 diagnostic codes, and prescription summaries while the doctor focuses purely on the patient. Emmanuel was brought on to completely rethink both the SaaS acquisition funnel and the in-clinic practitioner workstation.

The redesign prioritized cognitive calm: high-legibility typographic hierarchies, dark/light contrast optimization for clinical lighting conditions, and 1-click clinical review mechanisms that turn hours of administrative charting into seconds of confirmation.
