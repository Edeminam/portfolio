WITHIN EHR

HealthTech AI Clinical SaaS Design System HIPAA-Compliant
Visit Live Site (withinehr.com/redesign) ↗
Overview

The Brief & Clinical Challenge

Medical practitioners, psychologists, and outpatient clinic teams spend over 40% of their workday wrestling with antiquated, clunky electronic health record systems — leading to widespread physician burnout and diminished face-to-face patient connection.

WithinEHR integrates ambient AI audio intelligence to automatically generate accurate SOAP notes, ICD-10 diagnostic codes, and prescription summaries while the doctor focuses purely on the patient. Emmanuel was brought on to completely rethink both the SaaS acquisition funnel and the in-clinic practitioner workstation.

The redesign prioritized cognitive calm: high-legibility typographic hierarchies, dark/light contrast optimization for clinical lighting conditions, and 1-click clinical review mechanisms that turn hours of administrative charting into seconds of confirmation. The result covers scheduling, patient records, appointment management, billing, insurance, and an AI marketplace — all within a cohesive design system.

Marketing & Acquisition

SaaS Landing Page & Conversion Funnel

High-converting marketing page engineered to drive enterprise demo bookings. Features a bold AI-first value proposition, clear feature breakdowns, social proof sections, and a friction-minimal CTA hierarchy optimized through conversion research.

Plans & Monetisation

Pricing Plans & Subscription Architecture

Transparent, tiered pricing designed to reduce friction for solo practitioners and enterprise clinic groups alike. Feature comparison tables, clear per-provider billing breakdowns, and a 14-day free trial CTA drive confident purchasing decisions.

Clinical Operations

Appointment Requests & Prospect Management

Centralised intake dashboard tracking 245 total requests across Approved, Pending, and Rejected states with at-a-glance KPI cards. The table view supports multi-column filtering by patient, clinician, location type (Virtual / In-Office), and status for instant triage.

Calendar & Scheduling

Weekly Calendar — Active Schedule View

Live week-view calendar with colour-coded appointment statuses (Completed, In Progress, Checked-In, Attended, Cancelled, No Show). Supports My Calendar and Appointment List toggle, filter controls, and quick-create via the "+ Create" action.

Empty State Design

Weekly Calendar — Zero-State & Onboarding

Thoughtfully designed empty state for new practitioners onboarding to the platform. Clean, uncluttered grid preserves navigational affordances while reducing cognitive noise — guiding the clinician to create their first appointment without overwhelming prompts.

AI & Marketplace

AI Ambient Scribe — Marketplace Listing

Dedicated add-on detail page for the flagship AI Ambient Scribe tool ($25/provider/mo). Features structured plan details, HIPAA / SOC 2 Type II compliance badges, live ratings (4.7 ★ from 230 practices), integration tags (Encounter Notes, Orders), and real-world practice reviews — creating a trusted, evidence-based purchase experience.

Patient Records

Patient Profile & Insurance Management

Comprehensive patient record card (Julianne Vance) displaying personal identifiers, multi-tab navigation (Personal Info, Appointment, Documents, Payment, Insurance, Notes, Medication, Patient Portal), full insurance policy details, Claims History, insurance card uploads, and eligibility check triggers — all within a single, coherent screen.
