Overview

The Brief & National Mission

The Nigeria Police Force (NPF) provides critical statutory specialized services to individuals, embassies, and corporate organizations: character clearance certificates, firearms licensing, vehicle tint permits, and specialized protection details.

Prior to POSSAP, citizens spent days visiting zonal police headquarters in person, queueing for physical fingerprint cards and waiting weeks for manual paper verification. Unaccredited middle-men proliferated, undermining institutional trust and public safety record integrity.

Emmanuel was appointed to lead the user experience overhaul of the citizen portal and the internal law enforcement approval backoffice. The resulting platform enables citizens to apply online, select biometric appointment slots at local commands, track progress via SMS, and download cryptographically signed, verifiable digital certificates.
