NASARAWA CBTAS

Community-Based Tax Administration & Field Agent Revenue Platform

GovTech Tax Administration Field Agent POS
Visit Live Site (nasarawa.cbtas.com) ↗
Overview

The Brief & Revenue Challenge

The Nasarawa State Internal Revenue Service (NSIRS), operating under the executive mandate of Governor Engr. Abdullahi A. Sule ("Home of Solid Minerals"), faced systemic challenges with non-tax revenue collections across its 13 Local Government Areas (LGAs). Revenue from informal commerce, haulage, land registries, market kiosks, and transport unions was dominated by cash transactions and handwritten paper receipts—creating systemic revenue leakages and taxpayer friction.

The strategic mission was captured in the state's civic motto: "Pay your tax! Let's build Nasarawa State together." CBTAS (Central Billing and Tax Administration System) was commissioned to transform state tax operations into an all-digital, self-service civic infrastructure. The mandate required unifying invoicing, payment channels, receipt validation, and tax clearance into a singular, transparent platform.

As Lead UI/UX Designer, Emmanuel designed the citizen-facing web architecture and field officer flows: an intuitive 4-step progressive invoicing engine generating centralized CBTAS Invoice Numbers (CIN), an omnichannel payment checkout, real-time receipt authentication, and a cryptographically verifiable Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC) portal.

01 — Civic Governance Challenge & Digital Transformation

Eliminating Leakages through Centralized Invoicing

Across state ministries, departments, agencies (MDAs), and local councils, decentralized collection created blind spots that eroded citizen trust and drained state treasury reserves.

Legacy Revenue Pitfalls

The Fragmented Cash Reality

  • Vulnerable Cash Handling: Discretionary cash collection by field revenue personnel led to severe revenue losses and disputes.
  • Counterfeit Paper Receipts: Widespread circulation of fake handwritten receipts impossible for enforcement agents to authenticate.
  • Siloed Council Treasuries: Each of the 13 LGAs ran disconnected spreadsheets with zero real-time visibility into daily remittances.
  • Tedious Clearance Delays: Taxpayers waited weeks in physical revenue offices to verify payments and obtain physical TCC documents.
The CBTAS Solution

Centralized Digital Tax Infrastructure

  • Single CBTAS Invoice Number (CIN): Every bill originates from a centralized cryptographic number payable across any bank or channel.
  • Instant Cryptographic Validation: Live public validation tool to immediately authenticate official NSIRS digital receipts.
  • Direct Treasury Settlement: Card, USSD, and bank transfers settle straight into the Nasarawa State Consolidated Revenue Account.
  • Instant Self-Service TCC: 100% digital Tax Clearance Certificate verification with QR security codes for institutional third parties.
02 — Web Platform Interfaces

Desktop Architecture & Core Civic Modules

A cohesive, high-trust portal interface designed for diverse citizen demographics, corporate compliance teams, and state tax administrators.

Civic Portal & Gateway Storefront

Executive Credibility & Immediate Action Hub

The homepage opens with official Nasarawa State heraldry, the executive portrait of Governor Engr. Abdullahi A. Sule, and the civic charge: "Pay your tax! Let's build Nasarawa State together." The interface prioritizes high-frequency taxpayer tasks through four elevated action cards: Generate Invoice, Pay Bill, Validate Receipt, and Verify TCC. An integrated search engine and quick sign-in enable immediate taxpayer access without navigational friction.

4-Step Invoice Wizard

Progressive Tax Assessment Engine

Generating a state invoice was distilled into a clear, sequential 4-stage stepper: 1. Select User Type (Individual, Corporate, or Field Agent), 2. Payer Profile & MDA Selection, 3. Assessment Summary, and 4. Confirm & Generate CIN. Radio cards dynamically adapt required tax identifiers (NIN for citizens, RC/TIN for businesses) to prevent invalid submissions.

Omnichannel Settlement

Instant CIN Lookup & Secure Payment Gateway

Taxpayers input their CBTAS Invoice Number (CIN) to instantly retrieve line-item tax details, assessed ministry fees, and outstanding balances. Payments can be completed immediately via debit card, direct bank transfer, USSD, or at any commercial bank branch across Nigeria using the unified CIN reference.

Anti-Fraud Validation

Public Digital Receipt Authentication

To permanently eradicate fake and forged paper receipts, the receipt validation engine queries the central NSIRS database in real time. Citizens and road transport enforcement officers enter the receipt number or payment reference to instantly view tamper-proof settlement timestamps and official state verification.

Regulatory Compliance

Official Tax Clearance Certificate (TCC) Verification

State contractors, corporate tenders, and citizens requiring visa endorsements can verify TCC legitimacy online. By validating the certificate application number or TIN against NSIRS records, third parties authenticate verified fiscal compliance in seconds—eliminating bureaucratic delays and bribery avenues.

04 — Design System & GovTech Brand Architecture

Visual Identity, Color Authority & Inclusive Form Ergonomics

A civic digital identity balancing government institutional authority with consumer-grade fintech simplicity.

GovTech Palette

Nasarawa Civic Color Tokens

  • Emerald State Green (#15803D / #16A34A): The dominant civic authority color, rooted in the Nasarawa State flag and agricultural abundance.
  • Solid Minerals Ochre Gold (#D97706 / #CA8A04): High-priority transaction accent celebrating the "Home of Solid Minerals" state heritage.
  • Slate Canvas (#F8FAFC): Neutral, low-glare card backgrounds providing comfortable contrast for prolonged data entry.
  • Deep Authority Charcoal (#0F172A): Crisp typographical contrast exceeding WCAG 2.1 AA legibility across small displays.
Civic UX Principles

Inclusive Interaction Architecture

  • Plain-Language Tax Heads: Replaced obscure legislative revenue codes with familiar civic names (Vehicle Licensing, Market Stallage, Land Use).
  • Zero Pre-Authentication Barrier: Citizens can generate an invoice and settle dues without being forced to create a password beforehand.
  • Low-Bandwidth Resilience: Lightweight SVG icons, optimized WebP/PNG assets, and cached lookup schemas designed for 3G rural coverage.
  • Centralized CIN Traceability: Uniform 12-digit CBTAS Invoice Number (CIN) recognized seamlessly across banks, POS, and online gateways.
