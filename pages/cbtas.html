Overview

The Brief & Revenue Challenge

Nasarawa State Internal Revenue Service (NSIRS) was challenged by significant revenue leakage in informal market sectors, artisanal trade, and transport unions across its 13 Local Government Areas. Due to cash-based collections and handwritten receipts, millions in statutory revenue never reached state coffers.

CBTAS was envisioned as a complete digital ecosystem: a rugged mobile interface for field revenue officers, an SMS/card payment gateway for merchants, and a real-time web command center for state finance commissioners.

Emmanuel engineered the field agent mobile workflows for low-connectivity rural environments and crafted the central administrative portal with interactive geospatial heatmaps, daily collection tallies, and automated agent reconciliation.
